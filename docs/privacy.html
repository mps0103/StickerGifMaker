Privacy Policy

Effective 17 October 2026 · Applies to the DieCut app for Android

The short version. The app has no account, no sign-in and no server of ours. Your photos, videos, stickers and GIFs are processed on your phone and are never uploaded to us. The only data that leaves your device is what the advertising and purchase services described below collect when you use them.

1. Who we are

The app is published by MPS (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at info@dealtrix.com.

2. What stays on your device

To make a sticker or GIF the app works with the following, all of which is stored only on your phone:

  • the photos and videos you choose to edit;
  • the stickers, GIFs and sticker packs you create, including pack names, emoji tags and your publisher name;
  • your settings, such as your default border and whether haptic feedback is on;
  • a counter of how many free exports you have used this month;
  • a short log of recent error messages, used only if you choose to send feedback (see section 8).

We never receive any of this. We cannot see, recover or delete it for you, because we do not have it.

3. Access to your photos and videos

The app does not ask for access to your whole photo library. When you add photos it opens Android’s system photo picker, and the app receives only the items you select. Selected items are copied into the app’s private storage while you edit them, and the temporary previews are deleted automatically after about a day, or at any time with Settings → Temporary files → Clear.

When you tap Save to gallery, the app writes the picture you chose to your device’s Pictures folder. On Android 9 and earlier this uses the legacy storage permission, which Android requires for that one action.

4. Background removal happens on your phone

Cutting a subject out of a photo uses Google’s ML Kit subject segmentation, delivered through Google Play services. The processing runs on your device and your photo is not sent to us or uploaded for analysis. The first time you use it, Google Play services may download the model file from Google. That download contains no information about your photos.

5. Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob, including a banner on some screens and an optional rewarded video if you want an extra export. To show and measure ads, Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use information such as:

  • your Android advertising ID, IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information such as model, operating system version, language and app version;
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic information about the ad SDK.

This is collected by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google’s policies: How Google uses data in advertising and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in Android under Settings → Google → Ads. If you buy the ad-free upgrade (section 6) the app stops loading ads.

6. Purchases

The optional ad-free upgrade is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether your Google account owns the upgrade, so that it can switch ads off and lift the monthly export limit. Refunds and receipts are managed by Google Play.

7. WhatsApp and sharing

When you tap Add to WhatsApp, the app makes the chosen sticker pack available to WhatsApp through an Android content provider that only WhatsApp is permitted to read. Once WhatsApp has the pack, WhatsApp’s own privacy policy applies to it. When you choose Share on a sticker, the app you pick in Android’s share sheet receives that single file, under its own policy. The app is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by WhatsApp, Meta, Telegram or Google.

8. Feedback emails

If you tap Send feedback, the app opens a draft in your email app. The draft includes your app version, Android version, device make and model, and the last few error messages the app recorded. Nothing is sent unless you press send, and you can edit or delete any of it first.

10. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. We hold no personal information at all, but if you believe a child has used the app in a way that concerns you, contact us.

11. Keeping and deleting your data

Everything the app stores lives on your phone. Delete a sticker or pack inside the app to remove it, use Clear in Settings to remove temporary files, or uninstall the app to remove everything. Data held by Google (ads, Google Play) is managed in your Google account.

12. Your rights

Because we do not collect or store personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or erase. Depending on where you live, you may have rights over the data Google collects through advertising, and those requests go to Google through your Google account privacy controls.

13. Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change ships. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the updated policy.

14. Contact

Questions about this policy: info@dealtrix.com.
