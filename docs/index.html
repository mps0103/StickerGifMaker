Turn photos into stickers

An Android app. Everything runs on your phone: no account, no upload.

  • Pick a photo and the subject is cut out automatically; touch up the edge by hand if needed.
  • Add up to 20 photos at once and get a whole sticker pack in one go.
  • Add a border, shadow, caption or emoji tags, then add the pack to WhatsApp in one tap.
  • Make GIFs from videos or photos, and edit existing GIFs.
